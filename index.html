MARS MISSION

HOW TO PLAY

Your mission is to keep your crew alive on Mars!

💨

OXYGEN

Keep enough oxygen for your crew.

If oxygen runs out, your crew will suffocate and the mission will fail.
⚡

POWER

Manage your energy carefully.

Without power, life support stops working and the mission will fail.
🍎

FOOD

Make sure your crew has enough food.

No food means hunger, low energy and a failed mission.
☢️

RADIATION

Keep it LOW. Protect your crew from solar storms.

High radiation harms your crew. At 100% the mission fails.
🚀

GOAL

Survive all 3 levels: Easy, Medium and Hard.

Each level has 10 days, with 2 decisions every day. Every choice has a price!
